## What is AFL Masters? (`/what-is-afl-masters`)
- Full explainer + FAQ (verbatim facts):
  - AFL Masters (AFLM): male/female players 35+, safe/supportive/family-oriented, standardised modified
    rules acknowledged/supported by the AFL and Sport Australia. Governed by AFLM National Board + 9
    State/Territory Associations. Originated in Victoria in 1980; now 260+ teams across Australia.
  - National Carnival held annually — largest AFL Masters carnival event in Australia; players represent
    their state; must be 35+ to play in it.
  - **How to join:** email president@geelongaflmasters.com.au or Facebook Messenger.
  - **Age requirement:** generally 35+ (occasional exceptions); National Carnival strictly 35+.
  - **Team age groups:** Over 35's Seniors and Reserves, Over 45's, Over 55's.
  - **Modified rules:** reduce rough contact, increase fun factor — full detail at www.aflmasters.com.au
  - **Membership fees:** $350 New Players / $300 Current Players — includes registration, insurance,
    playing jumper, shorts, socks, game-day fees. Covers insurance, ground hire, equipment, trainers,
    umpires, incidentals. Payment plans available.
  - National Carnival: separate registration fee + optional merch (jumpers/shorts/socks) purchase.
  - Rules link mentioned ("Read our rules here") — target not captured (external aflmasters.com.au rules
    doc, not directly linked in scrape).
