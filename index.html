2026 President's Welcome

Where footy builds mateship and mental strength.

At Geelong Masters, we're more than just a footy club — we're a community built on mateship, inclusion, and genuine care for one another. While we still love the contest and the thrill of the game, our greatest win is the connection we create off the field.

We're proud to partner with Outside The Locker Room (OTLR) to support mental health and wellbeing across our club. It's not just about kicking goals — it's about checking in, looking after our mates, and making sure no one is ever left behind.

With players across Geelong and the Bellarine, our club throws open its arms to anyone aged 35 and up. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or lacing up for the first time in years, there's a spot here for you. We currently field four growing sides: Over 35s Seniors and Reserves, Over 45s, and Over 55s.

We train once a week, and over summer, our Tuesday sessions include our hit Summer 9's competition — fun, fast, and full of laughter. Once the season kicks in, we move to Wednesday evenings from 5pm at our home ground, Grinter Reserve, Newcomb. Training nights are a highlight, with post-training meals and cold drinks at the Newcomb Power Football Club — a chance to bond, unwind, and share a few laughs.

Whether you're looking to stay fit, relive the glory days, or simply find your tribe, Geelong Masters is your club. A place to play, connect, and look out for one another, on the field and off it.

Warm regards,
Paul “Swampy” Collis
President, Geelong AFL Masters · president@geelongaflmasters.com.au

Club communication

All the latest, on Team App.

As part of our commitment to keeping everyone informed, all official club communications now run through Team App. We'll still be active on social media and the website, but internal updates, important club news and event details are shared via Team App.

General group chats remain for casual discussions — but if you want to stay up to date with everything happening at the club, make sure you're on Team App. Download the app, search “Geelong AFL Masters,” and join the club. Partners and family members are welcome to join too — event updates and key info go out there as well.
